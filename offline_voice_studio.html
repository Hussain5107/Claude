Offline Voice Studio

Paste your script, generate a natural voice narration entirely in your browser using the open-source Kokoro neural voice model. The model downloads once (needs internet the first time) and is cached by your browser for fully offline use after that. Long scripts are exported as separate WAV parts as they finish, so you can start listening before the whole thing is done.
