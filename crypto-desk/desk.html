Survival Math

Leverage vs. liquidation

LeverageLiq. distance*Survives daily wick?
20×≈ 5%No — wicks hit first
10×≈ 10%Usually
5×≈ 20%Yes

Why you get liquidated at 20×: BTC/SOL routinely wick 2–5% intraday. At 20× your liquidation sits ~5% away — the wick reaches it before your planned stop. Dropping to 5–10× puts the stop back in control. Same levels, you just survive.

Position sizing — $40 account

Risk a fixed $1 per trade (2.5%). Position size = risk ÷ stop-distance%.

TradeStop dist.PositionMargin @10×
ETH long1.6%$63$6.3
SOL long0.9%$111$11.1
BTC break0.55%$182$18.2

Tighter stop = bigger position for the same $1 risk. That's the whole game: your loss is fixed, only the size changes. Never size by "how much can I afford to buy."

Session Rules
  • 1Wait for the trigger. If price hasn't broken the level, there is no trade. Watching is a position.
  • 2Stop goes on the exchange the moment you enter — never "in your head."
  • 3TP1 hit → move stop to breakeven. Now the trade is risk-free; let the runner go to TP2.
  • 4Two losses = done for the day. This single rule is what keeps a small account alive.
  • 5Max 5–10× leverage. Stick to BTC / ETH / SOL — skip low-cap alts, they wick-liquidate small accounts instantly.

These are reactive technical levels, not predictions. I read structure — I can't see the future. News (Fed, ETF flows, a whale dump) can invalidate any level in minutes.
