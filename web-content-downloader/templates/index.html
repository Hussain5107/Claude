What this does and doesn't do
  • Only download content you own, have permission to save, or that's public/openly licensed. Platform terms of service still apply to you, not just to this tool.
  • Private or age-restricted content, and anything behind a login, generally won't work — that's intentional, not a bug.
  • Scribd is not supported. Its documents sit behind a paywall/DRM-style viewer, and defeating that isn't something this tool does. If you have a subscription, use Scribd's own Download button instead.
